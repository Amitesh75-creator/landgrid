Questions

Do I need to come to India?

No. We work from scanned documents and you join the survey by video. If a Power of Attorney is needed, you sign it at the Indian consulate near you.

Which documents should I have ready?

Your registered deed, the latest land record for your state, any old survey sketch and recent tax receipts. Anything missing, we can apply for as certified copies.

What happens if someone encroaches?

We photograph it the same day and call you. With your approval we file a police complaint, send a legal notice through our advocate and support an injunction if needed.

Do you take control of my property?

Never. The land and every decision remain yours. Any Power of Attorney covers only the tasks you choose.
